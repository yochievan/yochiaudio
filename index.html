Our Heritage: From Precision Manufacturing to Acoustic Innovation

At Yochi Audio, we bridge the gap between decades of manufacturing heritage and the future of high-fidelity sound.

The Foundation

ZhongZhan Electronics

The bedrock of our expertise lies in ZhongZhan, our parent manufacturing facility in Dongguan. For over two decades, ZhongZhan has been the quiet force behind numerous international brands, operating as a specialist OEM partner.

With a legacy rooted in precision metalwork, the factory provides the robust infrastructure and MIL-STD-105E quality control standards that ensure every Yochi product is built to last.

The Breakthrough

Yochi Engineering Lab

Led by Lead Engineer Evan Zhao, Yochi Audio functions as a specialized technology gateway. Here we pushed the limits of AMT technology, resulting in our signature 12.5μm ultra-thin diaphragm—allowing response up to 50kHz.

The Feedback Loop

The Sette Brand

To ensure our engineering remains grounded in real-world performance, we operate Sette—our market-testing arm. By putting our units directly into the hands of audiophiles worldwide, we gather real-time data to inform our next generation of designs.
