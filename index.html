Yochi Audio serves as the "Acoustic Brain" and international engineering gateway for our group. Led by Lead Engineer Evan, we focus on pushing the boundaries of AMT technology—specifically our industry-leading 50kHz response and ultra-thin 12.5μm diaphragm series.

Our parent facility, ZhongZhan Electronics, provides the manufacturing foundation. With decades of precision OEM experience and MIL-STD quality control standards, ZhongZhan ensures that every R&D breakthrough is translated into reliable, high-capacity production at source factory costs.
